See how long a page is before you commit to reading it.

## How it works

- Open the extension from the toolbar to see the number of words on the page and in your selection.
- It estimates the reading time at about 230 words a minute.
- **Copy summary** puts a one-line summary on your clipboard.

## Good to know

- It only counts when you open the popup. It doesn't store or send what it reads.
- Pages that are already open need a reload after you add the extension.
