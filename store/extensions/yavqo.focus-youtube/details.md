Watch what you came for.

## What you can hide

- **Comments** under a video.
- **Recommended videos** next to and after a video.
- **Shorts** shelves and the Shorts link in the menu.

## Good to know

- It only runs on youtube.com and only changes how the page looks. It doesn't read your account or watch history.
- Each switch is saved on this Mac. Reload YouTube after adding the extension.
