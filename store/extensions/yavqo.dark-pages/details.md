Turns bright websites dark so they are easier on your eyes in the evening.

## How it works

- Flips the colours of the page, then flips photos, video and embedded frames back so they still look right.
- One switch in the toolbar popup turns it on or off. It applies to every site and is remembered on this Mac.

## Good to know

- It changes how every page looks, so it asks to **read and change data on all websites**. It never reads what is on the page and sends nothing anywhere.
- Sites that already have a dark theme can look odd. Switch Dark Pages off for them from the popup.
- Reload pages that are already open after you turn it on.
